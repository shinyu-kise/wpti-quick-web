# Short-bout guidance: scientific audit

Reviewed on 2026-10-30 for PR #10. This review concerns the optional guidance only. It does not validate WPTI-Quick or modify Quick v1.0 items, response categories, scoring, or score interpretation.

## Evidence checked

- [Bull et al., 2020, PMID 33239350](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33239350/), [WHO guideline publication](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240015128/): adult aerobic recommendations allow accumulation without a minimum 10-minute bout. Intensity and weekly volume still matter. Reducing sedentary time is a separate recommendation; light activity is not interchangeable with moderate-to-vigorous aerobic minutes.
- [Saint-Maurice et al., 2018, PMID 29567764](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29567764/): prospective observational analysis of accelerometer-measured moderate-to-vigorous activity in 4,840 US adults aged at least 40. The bout-pattern association does not show that light activity or any brief activity has the same effect. The UI avoids numerical risk estimates and causal claims.
- [Stamatakis et al., 2022, PMID 36482104](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36482104/), [publisher full text](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-022-02100-x): observational UK Biobank study of 25,241 nonexercisers. VILPA denotes vigorous daily-life activity; it is not a synonym for standing, light walking, or every short bout. Mortality associations do not establish an individual treatment effect. No mortality-risk percentage is presented in the app.
- [Dunstan et al., 2012, PMID 22374636](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22374636/): randomized acute crossover trial in 19 adults with overweight or obesity, comparing uninterrupted sitting with two-minute light or moderate walking breaks every 20 minutes. Post-meal glucose/insulin responses were the outcomes. The trial did not test standing alone, long-term mortality, or the app's optional timing examples.

## Changes from the draft

The original three PubMed identifiers matched their intended papers. The generic accumulation explanation was replaced in both languages with explicit distinctions between moderate-to-vigorous accumulation, vigorous daily-life activity, and light walking breaks. Dunstan's primary trial was added to document the separate acute evidence. The app states that its measurement properties and the behavioral or health effects of the suggestions remain unestablished.

The suggested five-minute, two-to-five-minute, and one-to-two-minute activities remain optional conversation examples, not validated doses, prescriptions, automatic goals, or score inputs. A participant's safety, mobility, health and necessary rest take priority. The sources do not establish efficacy of WPTI-Quick, its scoring, or this new guidance.

## Review boundary

This is a bounded check of the cited primary sources and their correspondence to the new text, not a systematic evidence review. Browser, privacy, scoring regression, print, CI and production checks are recorded separately by the release reviewer.
